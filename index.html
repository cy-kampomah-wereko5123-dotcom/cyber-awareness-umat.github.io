A Short Video on Sextortion & Cyberbullying Awareness

Section 1.0

Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the production of a short narrative video (30–50 minutes) aimed at raising awareness about sextortion and cyberbullying among university students. The video will combine realistic drama with informational resources to highlight the psychological and social impacts of these digital crimes, promote empathy, and educate viewers on prevention and response strategies. It is intended for campus-wide distribution via social media, orientation programs, and student wellness portals.

Section 2.0

Background & Rationale

Sextortion and cyberbullying are growing concerns in digital environments, particularly among young adults. Individuals aged 18–24 are at high risk of experiencing coercive online behaviors, which can lead to severe emotional distress, academic decline, and even self-harm. Many students are unaware of how to safely navigate these threats or where to seek help.

As digital natives, students are both the most vulnerable and the most influential audience for change. A visually engaging, peer-produced short film can effectively capture attention, foster discussion, and reduce stigma around reporting such incidents.

Section 4.0

Target Audience

The primary target audience are internet users, particularly those aged 18–30. Secondary audiences include tertiary institution students, student leaders, lecturers, and campus counseling units.

Section 5.0

Description of the Video Production

The proposed video will be a 30–50 minute short film produced by the team. It will combine dramatized scenes, narration, and clear calls to action — all crafted with a serious but relatable tone, avoiding graphic content.

Logline: A final-year mathematics prodigy must solve the most critical equation of his life after a private moment is stolen and weaponized for blackmail, threatening to erase his perfect future.

Concept Teaser

Tahiru's life is a model of elegant logic: months from graduation, top of his class, with a prestigious graduate position secured. His world is built on proof, predictability, and control — until a single compromised video leads to a brutal sextortion demand.

We'll witness a brilliant, analytical mind confront a crisis designed to exploit shame and silence. Will his logic save him, or will the pressure to maintain a flawless image destroy everything he's built? This short film explores the terrifying intersection of digital vulnerability, academic pressure, and the courage it takes to ask for help.

Section 7.0

Ethical Considerations

  • All actors will be fully informed of the sensitive content.
  • Script avoids graphic or triggering visual depictions.
  • Support resources will be displayed throughout and after the video.
  • Consent forms collected from all participants.
  • Advisor oversight to ensure accurate representation of psychological impacts.
Section 8.0

Conclusion

Sextortion and cyberbullying are real and growing threats within university communities. This proposed short video production provides a practical, creative, and impactful approach to educating students and promoting safer online behavior. By using student voices and realistic scenarios, the project aims to create awareness, inspire responsibility, and contribute to a more secure digital environment on campus.
